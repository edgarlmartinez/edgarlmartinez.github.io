Method

Segmentation. nnU-Net v2 (3D full resolution, the framework behind TotalSegmentator), one contrast-agnostic MRI model (T1 and T2FS/STIR pooled) and one CT model, trained on 40 patients. Post-processing: on MRI, a two-sequence consensus keeps the tumor components that T1 and T2FS/STIR agree on (falls back to the largest component when only one sequence exists); on CT, the largest connected component.

Normalization. CT stays in Hounsfield units, resegmented to −200…400 HU, fixed bin width 25 HU, resampled to 1×1×3 mm. MRI: N4 bias-field correction, z-score over the body, 3-SD resegmentation, fixed bin number 32, in-plane 1 mm and 2D texture because slices are 4–15 mm thick.

Features. PyRadiomics 3.1 (IBSI-aligned definitions): shape, first order, GLCM, GLRLM, GLSZM, GLDM, NGTDM on the original image.

Data and license

TCIA Soft-tissue-Sarcoma collection (Vallières M, et al. Phys Med Biol 2015;60:5471; Clark K, et al. J Digit Imaging 2013), obtained through the NCI Imaging Data Commons, CC BY 3.0. 51 patients with extremity soft-tissue sarcoma; expert GTV contours on T1, T2FS/STIR and the CT of the PET/CT. No BIDMC or patient data from this author's institution were used. The CT is the low-dose attenuation CT of PET/CT, so CT results are a lower bound for diagnostic CT.
